ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a field of computer science focused on building systems that can perform tasks associated with human intelligence.

AI systems can perform tasks such as pattern recognition, reasoning, language processing, prediction, and decision support.

Machine learning is an important branch of AI. Instead of manually programming every rule, machine learning systems learn patterns from data.

Artificial intelligence is applied in healthcare, education, finance, transportation, customer service, and scientific research.
